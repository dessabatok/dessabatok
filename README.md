### 📊 Big Data for Official Statistics in Latin America and the Caribbean *(Ongoing)* 🚧

An ongoing research project examining how **National Statistical Offices across Latin America and the Caribbean** are using Big Data to produce and support **official statistics**. The project focuses on mapping current practices, data sources, applications, and challenges related to the adoption of Big Data within national statistical systems.

**Topics:** Big Data · Official Statistics · National Statistical Offices · Latin America and the Caribbean · Data Science · Public Policy

[View project](https://hub.ibge.gov.br/consulta.htm)
